bandit12:
https://mayadevbe.me/posts/overthewire/bandit/level13/
Concepts: gzip, bzip2, tar, xxd. Had to look at the hexdump using xxd file.
  first bytes show file type (every file has a unique hex signature https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_file_signatures).
  425a for bzip, 1f8b for gzip, 2nd col 7461 for tar (or .bin on the final col)
  so decompressing with either gzip or bzip2 you get data, then look with xxd to determine next method.
  #gzip, bzip, tar need either their extension or bin at the end
